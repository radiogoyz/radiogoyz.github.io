The album cover for The Newer Shit was designed by Jude Clubber, it features him standing on water while smoking a joint (devils lettuce). In his ears are crucifix earings, this is to represent the fact that even though we hear good we still speak evil.

Jude Clubber Has Leprosy is the first single ever released by The Radio Goyz

The single is the first and only single released for the album The Newer Shit, it contains upon it four tracks

  1. UNCLUBBABLE
  2. Jude Clubber Has Leprosy
  3. Jude Has Herpes [REMIX]
  4. Policestate

lyricist: Jude / Unclubber
Instruments: Jude / Unclubber / Shoeman

producer: Shoeman

Jude Clubber Has Leprosy was originally supposed to be a 3 track single, then a 6 track EP including Geigercounter (hence why geigercounter was never released as a single), however the idea was scrapped and Jude Clubber Has Leprosy is now a 4 track single.

The album cover features a headshot of Unclubber in a forest, the forest pictured is where the band hangs out to write songs.
