The Radio Goyz is a 4 man band comprised of:

Frontman Jude Clubber
Lyricist and Drummer: Unclubber
Producer: Shoeman
and Edward

The Radio Goyz was formed when Shoeman and Jude met at an party held the week before their king was overthrown. After becoming quick friends and being forced to flee the country. Jude and Shoeman along with Judes two other friends; Unclubber and Edward formed a band called The Radio Goyz.

With only one album out (The Newer Shit) as of july 2026, The Radio Goyz are still in their infancy.
